[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#FF8C00]🌪️ What is TNHoppers?[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

Are traditional vanilla hoppers dragging your server's [B]TPS[/B] down and inflating [B]MSPT[/B] into the red zone? Do massive mob farms, sugarcane towers, and item sorters with hundreds of hoppers lag your Skyblock or Survival realm?

[B]TNHoppers[/B] is a high-performance, next-generation [B]Wireless Hopper & Suction System[/B] built from the ground up for modern [B]Paper and Purpur 1.20 - 1.21.x[/B] servers. Designed by [B]Tài Nguyễn (TN)[/B], it completely eliminates laggy vanilla hopper chains by routing items wirelessly through a budgeted, event-driven engine backed by a zero-allocation live view cache and SQLite WAL storage.

[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#00BFFF]🔥 Key Features at a Glance[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]⚡ 2 Specialized Hopper Types:[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Normal Hopper (Radius Mode):[/B] Collects free-falling ground items within a customizable spherical radius (upgradable from [B]3 to 16 blocks[/B]). Perfect for compact farms, mob grinders, and private storage rooms.
[*][B]Chunk Hopper (Full-Chunk Vacuum):[/B] Instantly vacuums [B]EVERY[/B] dropped item across the entire [B]16×16 chunk[/B] from bedrock to sky limit! Runs with a chunk-wide entity cache that calls [I]getEntities()[/I] exactly once per tick cycle regardless of how many hoppers reside in that chunk.
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🔒 Brand New: Break & Explosion Protection for Linked Chests:[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Tamper-Proof Containers:[/B] When a chest, barrel, or shulker is linked to a TNhopper, it [B]CANNOT be broken or stolen[/B]! Breaking attempts are cancelled with a clear sound and warning message instructing the player to unlink via GUI first.
[*][B]Double Chest Normalization:[/B] Automatically protects both the left and right halves of a double chest.
[*][B]Explosion Immunity:[/B] Linked hoppers and destination chests are 100% immune to TNT, Creeper, Wither, and crystal blast damage!
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]📦 Brand New: Upgradable Virtual Deep Storage (10k to 250k Items):[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Massive Virtual Storage:[/B] Say goodbye to giant walls of chests! Linked containers gain a virtual buffer starting at [B]10,000 items[/B], upgradable to [B]50,000[/B], [B]100,000[/B], and [B]250,000 items[/B]!
[*][B]Automatic Overflow:[/B] When the physical container is 100% full, items seamlessly overflow into the virtual storage buffer.
[*][B]Smooth Auto-Drain:[/B] When slots open in the physical chest, items smoothly drain back into physical slots.
[*][B]Deep Storage Viewer GUI:[/B] Middle-click any linked chest to view, inspect, and withdraw items (1 item, 64 items, or all) or click [B]Drain Now (Slot 53)[/B].
[*][B]Anti-Loss Guarantee:[/B] Unlinking is prevented if the virtual buffer contains items, and state is persisted to SQLite WAL.
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🪄 Wireless Link Wand & Smart Routing:[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Link hoppers directly to target containers (Chests, Trapped Chests, Barrels, Shulkers) using the [B]Link Wand[/B] ([I]/tnhoppers wand[/I]).
[*][B]Two Transfer Modes:[/B] Sequential priority ([I]INSERT_BY_ORDER[/I]) or round-robin ([I]DISTRIBUTE_EQUALLY[/I]).
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🧙‍♂️ Auto-Sorter Wizard (Slot 48):[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]One-Click Sorting & Renaming:[/B] Place sample items in your hotbar and click Slot 48 in the Links GUI to automatically configure Whitelist/Blacklist filters across all linked chests and automatically rename them!
[*][B]100% Non-Mutating:[/B] Player inventory is strictly read-only. Includes overwrite confirmation prompts and automatic rollback.
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🏷️ Custom Chest Renaming (Slot 47):[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Assign friendly names to your linked chests (e.g. [I]Ore Chest, Farm Drops, Trash Bin[/I]) directly in chat or via Shift-Click in GUI.
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]📦 Visual Per-Link GUI Filtering (54-Slot Chest):[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Whitelist & Blacklist Modes:[/B] Click any linked chest in the GUI to open a full 54-slot container. Drop item samples right in!
[*][B]Strict ItemMeta Matching:[/B] Toggle strict mode to accurately match custom display names, lore, enchantments, and potion effects.
[*][B]Filter Clipboard (Copy & Paste):[/B] Copy a filter profile with 1 click and paste it across multiple destination chests instantly.
[*][B]Auto-Void Unmatched:[/B] Enable the trash-voiding bucket (Slot 46) to automatically destroy unmatched items so hoppers never clog!
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]💎 5-Branch Interactive Upgrades GUI:[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]⚡ Speed:[/B] Reduces transfer delay from 20 ticks down to 15, 10, 5, or 2 ticks/transfer.
[*][B]📦 Amount:[/B] Increases items moved per transfer from 1 up to 4, 16, 32, or 64 items.
[*][B]🛢️ Deep Storage Capacity (Slot 13):[/B] Increases virtual buffer from 10,000 up to 250,000 items/chest!
[*][B]🔗 Max Links:[/B] Expands simultaneous linked chest connections from 1 up to 15 chests.
[*][B]📏 Distance & Radius:[/B] Expands suction radius up to 16 blocks and wireless linking distance up to 250 blocks.
[*]Fully supports [B]Vault Economy[/B] with automatic fallback to [B]XP Levels[/B].
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🛡️ Deep Island Protection (SuperiorSkyblock2 Ready):[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Checks island permissions dynamically: players must hold [B]BUILD[/B] privileges at the hopper and [B]CHEST[/B] privileges at the destination container.
[*][B]Cross-Island Lockdown:[/B] Blocks players from routing items across island boundaries.
[*][B]Real-time Member Validation:[/B] If a player is kicked or banned from an island, their hoppers immediately freeze.
[*][B]NoOp Fallback:[/B] Works seamlessly as a standalone plugin on non-Skyblock servers (Survival, SMP, Towny).
[/LIST]

[*][B][COLOR=#FFD700]🚀 Rock-Solid Technical Architecture:[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]PDC Level Preservation:[/B] When hoppers are broken or exploded by TNT/Creeper, they drop with all upgrade levels and hopper type saved inside [I]PersistentDataContainer[/I]. Placing it back restores all stats immediately!
[*][B]Zero-Allocation Live View (activeHoppersView()):[/B] Traverses active hoppers with a weakly consistent iterator, eliminating garbage collection spikes.
[*][B]Symmetrical Transactional Rollback:[/B] Both container slot arrays and world entity item stacks are snapshot-protected against unexpected server lag or event cancellation.
[*][B]SQLite WAL Single-Writer:[/B] Asynchronous single-writer queue with 5000ms busy timeout and [I]PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)[/I] shutdown verification.
[*][B]19 Automated Unit Tests:[/B] 100% verified test coverage for mass conservation and cache consistency.
[/LIST]
[/LIST]
